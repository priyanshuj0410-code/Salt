Salt: Owner IA, simplified & natural

A redesigned information architecture shaped to how the owner actually thinks. Covering every touchpoint, in far fewer places.

S
Santosh: Owner · the persona this IA is shaped for

Watches more than he operates. Types little, reads numbers not menus, judges a tool in 60 seconds by typing a drug name. Thinks in khaata: who owes me, what I owe. His bar for "instant" is PhonePe; his ledger model is Khatabook; his channel is WhatsApp; his truth is the bank SMS. The internet drops, so billing must never stop.

Today the app is 13 modules organised the way the data is built. The owner doesn't think in modules: he thinks in five questions: how is my shop right now, who owes me, what do I have, what am I selling/buying, and am I covered. So the natural IA is one Home that answers "how's my shop", five areas that match his mental model, and a Setup drawer tucked out of the daily path: with the old Reports silo dissolved into context, Money unified into one khaata, and Compliance unified into one place. Nothing is removed; everything is regrouped. Click a rail item to jump to its area.
