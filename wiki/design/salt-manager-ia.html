Rohit — Manager · the operational spine, and the would-be champion

Runs the floor and approves the exceptions; does the heavy entry himself (GRN, returns); the most fluent across the whole app. Interrupted constantly — needs to resume mid-task. Benchmarks the app against fintech apps. His killer view is seeing all counters live; his daily friction is approvals that should be faster than a phone call. Has everything except Settings.

Base = the Owner IA. Same spine — one Home, five work areas (Sell · Buy · Stock · Money · Comply), type-to-find, numbers-first, offline + WhatsApp + en-IN, Hinglish labels. This page only shows what changes for the manager: Home becomes the live floor, Approvals graduate to a first-class Inbox, Setup is removed, and the five areas turn from observational (owner watches) into operational (manager acts).
