The limit case — where the information architecture all but disappears.

V
Vijay — Cashier · the fast biller; trained in 3 days, high turnover

Pure muscle memory and speed. Reads almost nothing — navigates by position, rhythm, colour and sound. An English dialog freezes him; a moved field costs days. The printed bill is “done”. His apps are Reels, BGMI and UPI — he learns combos by repetition, and his bar for “completed” is the UPI green tick + sound. He doesn’t understand GST or stock. He bills.

Base = Owner + Manager + Accountant + Pharmacist. The pharmacist already made the Home a working screen; the cashier takes it to the limit. There is no navigation, no dashboard, no areas — the counter is the entire app. The series’ “one persistent surface per role” degenerates, because the whole app is the one action. His “IA” isn’t a tree of places — it’s a keystroke sequence; and the only thing reaching beyond his screen is an escalation to the manager, which never navigates him away.
