The finale: where the base forks: not the desktop nav at all, but a separate offline-first mobile app.

K
Karan: Field Salesman · on a bike, on his beat; tracked today only as data

The one role not at a counter, not on Marg, not on a keyboard. He rides a beat of 15-40 chemists: pitches schemes, takes orders, delivers, collects cash. Today his system is a paper pad + WhatsApp + memory. Phone-native (UPI, Maps, WhatsApp) but ERP-illiterate. Offline is his default state, and his aspiration is a delivery-partner app, list, tap, done, signal or no signal.

Base = the five desktop IAs (Owner → Cashier), as reference, not inheritance. The field salesman is where the spine forks. Everything the set assumed: a keyboard, Marg muscle memory, a counter, a left rail, g+letter, ⌘K: is gone. So this is a separate offline-first mobile app modelled on a delivery-partner app (Swiggy / Udaan), not an ERP. What carries over is only abstract: numbers-first, Home answers his first question, one persistent surface, his-slice-only, en-IN & WhatsApp. And he plugs back into the other five: his order becomes the shop's order, his collection hits the accountant's books and his own commission.

Why a separate app, not a screen in the ERP

He has no terminal, no keyboard and no Marg habit to build on. Bolting him onto the desktop nav would fail on day one. He needs a thumb-driven, one-handed, offline-first app he can use at a chemist's counter with one bar of signal. The form factor he already lives in (WhatsApp, Maps, UPI, the delivery-partner apps).

⟳
His persistent surface: the sync + cash strip
Always on top. What's saved offline vs synced, and his cash-in-hand, because for him, "is it synced?" is a question about his money at day-end. (The manager had an Inbox; the accountant an Export; Karan has this trust strip.)
🗺
Home = the Beat
His first question is "where am I and what's the next stop: and what do they owe?" The route list is his home, like a rider's order queue.
🔗
It plugs into the shop
Order → syncs in as the shop's sales order. Collect → posts to the accountant's books + his commission. The owner's credit limit gates his booking; an over-limit booking pings the manager's Inbox.

The big moves: how the base forked for this persona
Built by forking the five desktop IA bases, from the field-salesman persona (bike · beat · offline-default · paper-pad-today · delivery-partner aspiration) and his place in the lifecycle (S7 order · S10 deliver · S12 collect, the road, outside the shop walls). A design proposal, not a spec.
