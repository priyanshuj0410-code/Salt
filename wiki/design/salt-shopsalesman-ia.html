Deepak: Shop Salesman · the product-knowledge & relationship person at the counter

Knows the rack by heart, grows the basket, and never says nahi hai. Greets the walk-in or reads the chemist's slip, finds the product, quotes price + scheme, offers a substitute when something's out, and upsells the add-on. Strong on brand names, quick mental math, weak UI English. No distinct login: he works the counter, and he aspires to run his own shop or become a company MR.

Base = all six prior IAs. He doesn't invent a new structure. He recombines two counter patterns and re-points them commercially. From the cashier: the counter is the app, escalate-don't-navigate, offline, stable. From the pharmacist: a persistent Find surface. His one new contribution is the unified lookup: stock + rack location + price + scheme + substitute + upsell in a single result: and a commercial lens (basket, schemes, the regular). He is the selling counter to the cashier's billing counter and the pharmacist's clinical counter.
Three lenses on one counter. The synthesis

One physical counter, three roles, three lifecycle stages. The IA gives each the same surface with a different lens: and they hand off to one another in sequence: sell → gate → bill.
