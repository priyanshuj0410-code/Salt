The same base — but her Home is the counter, and compliance stops being a place.

A
Anjali — Pharmacist (D.Pharm) · the registered name on the licence; legally must be present

Thinks in salts — iska salt kya hai — molecule first, brand second. Substitutes on instinct when stock is out; checks the strip for expiry by habit. Her gold standard is Tata 1mg’s salt search; customers send the Rx as a WhatsApp photo. Her defining behaviour: under a long queue she skips the H1 register — the gap that hurts at inspection. Clinical, not commercial.

Base = Owner + Manager + Accountant. She inherits the spine, type-to-find, offline + en-IN + Hinglish (owner), and the two cross-role patterns from the manager & accountant: Home answers the role’s first question, and each role gets one persistent action surface (manager → Inbox, accountant → Export, pharmacist → Salt search). Two things change hard for her: her Home is a working counter, not a dashboard, and compliance becomes an inline gate, not a destination — while the entire commercial half of the app falls away.
