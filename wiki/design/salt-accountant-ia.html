B
Banwari: Accountant / Munimji · evening-shift, often shared across shops

Thinks in Dr/Cr, vouchers and ledgers, not screens. Keeps a parallel Excel and distrusts auto-totals until they tie to the paisa. Batches a week of entries at once, often back-dated. Strong accounting English, weak UX English. Excel is his real workplace; his fears are a GST mismatch notice and ITC reversal. He owns the back end of the chain, settle & comply.

Base = the Owner + Manager IAs. He inherits the spine, numbers-first, type-to-find, Reports-in-context, en-IN & Hinglish (from the owner), and two manager patterns: Home answers the role's first question, and each role gets one persistent action surface (manager → Inbox; accountant → Export). What changes is the scale: operations disappear: there's no Sell/Buy/Stock to run. So the whole IA collapses to a Desk, the Books, and the Tax.
